# LIFT treatment consultation pages — content notes

These pages explain the clinician's supplied treatment groupings, not a mandatory sequence or a personalized recommendation. They supplement a consultation rather than replacing procedure-specific discussion, suitability assessment, consent or aftercare.

## Tissue Remodeling & Tightening

QuantumRF is described as delivering RF beneath the skin for soft-tissue contraction. Morpheus8 is described as RF microneedling for tissue remodeling and skin texture/firmness. They are presented as alternatives selected by assessment, matching the user's protocol.

Primary references:
- InMode, QuantumRF: https://www.inmodemd.com/technologies/quantumrf
- InMode, Morpheus8: https://www.inmode.com.au/pages/morpheus8

## Structural Support & Biological Optimization

Sculptra and Radiesse are grouped as biostimulators. Sculptra's gradual collagen response is distinguished from immediate contour support. Radiesse wording is conditional because its role and volumizing effect depend on preparation and use. No specific dilution, site, dose, treatment schedule, duration or jurisdictional approval claim is made.

Primary references:
- Galderma, Sculptra: https://www.sculptrausa.com/discover-sculptra
- Merz, Radiesse mechanism: https://radiesse.com/professionals/about/moa/
